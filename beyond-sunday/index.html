Beyond Sunday

A Sunday series about the other six days.

Grace Covenant's November series is built on James 1:22, about putting Sunday's message to work through the week. So I built the identity around the week itself. On the LED wall, Monday through Saturday columns frame every Sunday slide, and the sermon slide splits into seven columns, one for each day.

The package covers seven service moments, from walk-in to walk-out, each in three formats: the 3840×1080 LED wall, 16:9 screens and lower thirds. Layered Photoshop files let the church's team update the copy.
